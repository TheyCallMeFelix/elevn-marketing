Frequently asked questions

What is Elevn?

Elevn is the customer-facing Business Operating System from ElevenStack LLC. It connects payments, invoices, estimates, customers, forms and everyday business tools.

Do I need a separate payment processor?

Elevn is designed around integrated payment processing. Payment acceptance requires merchant approval, compatible gateway activation and any applicable processing terms.

Can customers pay online or in person?

Yes. Eligible merchants can use hosted invoice payment links, configured payment pages, a virtual terminal and compatible connected card readers. Tap to Pay on iPhone is not part of the current offering.

Which plan includes text-to-pay?

Text-to-pay is a Growth-tier capability and is not included in Core. Transactional messages should only be sent to customers with appropriate consent.

Can I import existing customers?

Customer CSV import is offered with Growth. Customer records and duplicate review tools help organize your existing customer list. Other capabilities depend on plan access.

Does Elevn support multiple locations?

The Multi-Location plan is designed for separate authorized business workspaces, location switching and location-level reporting.

Are software and payment processing charges the same?

No. Software subscriptions and payment processing are separate. Processing terms and compatible hardware depend on merchant setup.

What happens to existing ElevenStack accounts?

Elevn is the evolution of the existing software. The planned migration is designed to preserve merchant accounts, billing and customer records. Continue using your existing ElevenStack workspace until official migration instructions are provided.

Can I sign up for Elevn now?

Elevn is in pre-launch. You can explore the product, compare plans, open the live Elevn demo, or try an illustrative walkthrough. Signup and subscription checkout are available through the Elevn application.
